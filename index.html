About CareerConnect

CareerConnect is a dedicated online job portal created as a BCA Internet and Web Technology project. Our mission is to bridge the gap between talented college students looking for internships or entry-level jobs and companies seeking skilled freshers.

  • ✔ Dedicated Student & Recruiter Dashboards
  • ✔ Direct PDF Resume Uploads & Downloads
  • ✔ Transparent Real-time Application Status
